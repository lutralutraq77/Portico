The [canonical acceptance plan](../ACCEPTANCE_TEST_PLAN.md) and [manifest](../tests/acceptance/manifest.json) contain 91 distinct runtime cases. Seven are implemented; 84 remain planned. The manifest links executable scenarios; actual passing evidence is specific to the source and run. Socket/process scenarios execute only in the guarded NIC-less Linux guest, with exclusions/skips on other hosts reported separately. A policy denial response cannot replace observed destination socket counts. Virtual authenticators cannot establish physical hardware backing or independent custody. Cross-compilation cannot prove a platform's keystore, lifecycle or local-user boundary. A quarantined encrypted restore cannot prove a complete recovery ceremony. These requirements remain part of the objective.

Current software checks are scripts/check.ps1 (both Go modules, unit/race/fuzz, static/dependency/secret/workflow/documentation checks, builds and SBOMs) and scripts/test-linux.ps1 (isolated Linux kernel, guest loopback only).
